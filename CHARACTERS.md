# BOARDING HOUSE: Key characters

These are the people your story is about. Status: **draft for approval.**

- **15 classmates** start JSS1 with you. One more joins in JSS2, and one in SS1.
- **3 key seniors** and **2 key juniors** complete the cast.
- Every class around them is filled with background students (GAME_DESIGN.md §4).

**How to read a card**
- **Relationship hooks:** what raises or lowers your bond.
- **Arc:** optional beats by year. ➜ marks a branch your choices decide.
- **Exits:** ways the character can leave school before graduation.
- **Where are they now:** endings, picked by their arc state and your relationship.
- **Voice:** goes into the AI conversation prompt; preset lines use the same voice.

**Gender rule:** characters tied to your hostel (bunkmate, school parent, wicked senior, school child) take your gender. Names marked ⚥ work for either; others list both versions.

## 1. Dami Adeyemi ⚥: the best friend candidate (bunkmate)

- **Look and growth:**
  - **JSS1:** small, shirt too big ("you'll grow into it"), always slightly crumpled.
  - **JSS3:** growth spurt, taller than you.
  - **SS3:** neat, confident, with a signature wristwatch from their dad.
- **Personality:** warm, loyal, homesick at first, funny once comfortable, conflict-avoidant.
- **Voice:** mixes Pidgin and English. Calls you "my person". Says "Abeg" a lot. Laughs at their own jokes.
- **Backstory:** only child; their mum is a nurse, their dad works in Abuja.
- **Goal:** to make it through and become a doctor like their mum's colleagues.
- **Secret:** their parents are quietly separating (revealed in JSS3).
- **Relationship hooks:**
  - ↑ sharing food, sitting together, defending them, keeping secrets.
  - ↓ ignoring them for the "cool" crowd, telling their secret.
- **Arc: "My Person"**
  - **JSS1:** cries the first night; you comfort them (or not). Last Card under the bunk becomes your thing.
  - **JSS2:** Dami gets pulled into Zara's circle ➜ you join, you sulk, or you invite Zara into yours.
  - **JSS3:** the divorce. Dami stops eating and their grades fall. ➜ Notice it (talk freely; the AI knows the beat) and support them, or miss it and Dami pulls away.
  - **SS1:** your departments split you (if you pick differently): a test of the friendship.
  - **SS2:** a big falling-out over a crush (Tams) or a betrayal (if you told the secret) ➜ make up, or stay apart.
  - **SS3:** you sign each other's shirts first, or not at all.
- **Exits:** none (always stays), unless the friendship hits rock bottom; then they transfer in SS2.
- **Where are they now:**
  - Paediatric doctor in Ibadan, "still calls you their person";
  - Pharmacist, "you lost touch, but they kept your slam book";
  - Moved abroad, "sends you a voice note every birthday".

## 2. Chidera "Dera" Okafor ⚥: the rival for 1st position

- **Look and growth:** glasses from JSS1 and always ironed. By SS3: debate blazer, posture like a lawyer.
- **Personality:** brilliant, competitive, proud, secretly kind; hates losing more than they love winning.
- **Voice:** precise Nigerian English with debate phrases: "Point of correction." "Technically…".
- **Backstory:** their father is a professor who accepts nothing below first position.
- **Goal:** first in every term, then a scholarship abroad.
- **Secret:** panic attacks before exams; Dera hides in the toilet and counts tiles.
- **Relationship hooks:**
  - ↑ beating them fairly, studying together, respecting their brains.
  - ↓ cheating, mocking their pressure.
  - Rivalry rises whenever you outscore them.
- **Arc: "First Position"**
  - **JSS1 T1:** the first report card shows Dera 1st and you somewhere ➜ the rivalry begins if you finish in the top 3.
  - **JSS2:** a joint quiz-competition team ➜ win together, or blame each other.
  - **JSS3 BECE:** Dera is offered leaked "expo" ➜ report it, join them, or talk them out of it (callback: "You stopped me in JSS3").
  - **SS1:** you find Dera mid-panic attack in the toilet ➜ help and keep the secret, or tell people.
  - **SS2:** a scholarship interview; Dera asks you to mock-interview them.
  - **SS3:** valedictorian decided by WAEC results and character; the speech mentions you (good or bad).
- **Exits:** none.
- **Where are they now:**
  - PhD at Cambridge, "dedicated the thesis to their study partner";
  - Senior Advocate in training, "still says 'point of correction' at dinner";
  - Burnt out, then found peace teaching, "the kindest Maths teacher in Enugu".

## 3. Babajide / Bolanle "Bulldozer" Lawal: the bully

- **Look and growth:** big for their age; repeating JSS1 makes them the oldest. Shirt tight across the shoulders. By SS3, either a calm giant or a scarred troublemaker.
- **Personality:** loud, intimidating and insecure. Funny when not defensive. Fiercely loyal once won.
- **Voice:** street Pidgin, short and threatening: "You dey whine me?" Softer and slow when trusting.
- **Backstory:** dad is a mechanic with a quick temper; Jide was hit at home and hits at school.
- **Goal:** to not be "the dull one" any more.
- **Secret:** **dyslexia**. Jide can barely read and pretends their eyes hurt.
- **Relationship hooks:**
  - ↑ standing up to them (respect), helping them read secretly (trust), never mocking them.
  - ↓ snitching, mocking their reading.
- **Arc: "Bulldozer"**
  - **JSS1:** takes your bucket and Strongman ➜ fight back, report them, or hand it over.
  - **JSS1–2:** you find Jide struggling to read a punishment letter ➜ help in secret (the key beat), laugh, or tell people.
  - **JSS2:** if you told the house master earlier, Jide remembers: *"Remember when you snitched on me in JSS2?"*.
  - **JSS3:** Jide passes BECE only if you helped them read ➜ otherwise they repeat again.
  - **SS1:** a fight with Commander ➜ you back them up, or Jide is expelled.
  - **SS2–3:** if redeemed, the **Labour Prefect** who protects juniors, and you.
- **Exits:** expelled in SS1, or repeats JSS3 (leaves your set).
- **Where are they now:**
  - Owns the biggest auto-workshop in Lagos, "named a bay after you";
